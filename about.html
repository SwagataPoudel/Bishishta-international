About Us

Empowering Communities with Purpose & Passion

Bishistha International is a non-governmental organization dedicated to uplifting lives through education, advocacy, and empowerment. We work towards equality, compassion, and sustainable progress for all.

Our Mission & Vision

We believe in empowering marginalized communities by promoting human rights, education, gender equality, and sustainable development. Our vision is to create a society where everyone lives with dignity and hope.

Our Mission

To promote equality, justice, and opportunity for all through empowerment and advocacy.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate world where human rights and social justice are a lived reality.

Our Values

Integrity, Inclusiveness, Compassion, and Commitment to Positive Change.
